\begin{abstract}

随着三维传感与建模技术的快速发展，开放集三维表征学习逐渐成为支撑虚拟现实、智能制造等领域的核心技术。传统方法多基于封闭集假设，但是现实场景需处理动态开放的未知类别。因此，如何构建对未见类的泛化表征，并在多模态融合中提升开放环境鲁棒性，成为当前三维视觉研究的关键问题。开放集三维表征学习旨在构建通用化特征空间，既能区分已知类别，又可自适应扩展至未知物体，同时保障多模态融合的鲁棒性。三维物体检索作为验证表征有效性的典型下游任务，要求模型不仅能识别已知类，还需有效处理未知类别物体，兼顾识别准确率与泛化能力。

为此，本文提出一种融合视觉大模型与多模态结构信息的开放集三维表征学习框架。基于HGM²R模型，保留其对点云和体素的建模能力，同时，引入DINOv2视觉大模型替代原MVCNN模块，以提取高质量多视图语义嵌入。通过冻结DINOv2参数，将其输出的语义嵌入与PointNet、VoxNet提取的几何特征拼接融合，形成统一多模态嵌入。进一步，利用超图神经网络建模样本间高阶结构关系，并设计结构一致性对比损失与记忆锚点对齐机制，优化嵌入空间的判别性与开放性。

在OS-MN40-core等四个开放集标准数据集上的实验表明，本方法在mAP、Recall@100等核心指标上均优于现有方法，尤其在复杂结构分布数据上，DINOv2的语义增强与几何拓扑建模的协同效应显著提升了对未知类的感知能力。消融实验验证了多模态联合学习的必要性。本研究实现了大模型与结构感知机制的协同创新，探索了自监督视觉知识向三维场景迁移的路径，兼具工程可扩展性与理论价值。

\end{abstract}

Open set 3D representation learning has gradually become the core technology supporting virtual reality, intelligent manufacturing and other fields. Traditional methods are mostly based on the closed set assumption, but real-world scenarios need to deal with dynamically open unknown categories. Therefore, how to construct a generalized representation of unseen classes and improve the robustness of the open environment in multimodal fusion has become a key issue. It aims to construct a generalized feature space, which can not only distinguish known categories, but also adaptively extend to unknown objects, while ensuring the robustness of multimodal fusion. 3D object retrieval requires the model to not only recognize known classes, but also effectively process unknown types of objects, taking into account accuracy and generalization ability.

To this end, this paper proposes an open-set 3D representation learning framework that integrates visual large models and multimodal structure information. Based on the HGM²R model, it retains its modeling capabilities for point clouds and voxels, and at the same time, the DINOv2 visual large model is introduced to replace the original MVCNN module to extract high-quality multi-view semantic embeddings. By freezing the DINOv2 parameters, the output semantic embedding is spliced and fused with the geometric features extracted by PointNet and VoxNet to form a unified multimodal embedding. Furthermore, the supergraph neural network is used to model the higher-order structural relationship between samples, and the structural consistency contrast loss and memory anchor alignment mechanism are designed to optimize the discriminant and openness of the embedded space.

Experiments on four open-set standard datasets such as OS-MN40-core show that the proposed method is superior to the existing methods in core indicators such as mAP and Recall@100, especially in complex structure distribution data, and the synergistic effect of DINOv2's semantic enhancement and geometric topology modeling significantly improves the perception of unknown classes. Ablation experiments verify the necessity of multimodal joint learning.
